# Practice a professional workflow

Open [Professional practice](/studio/practice/professional) to work through a fictional case without a provider key. Each case starts with a fixed source packet and three steps. The second answer has a deliberate mistake for you to correct. Continue to see a fixed authored reference answer and compare it with your own revision. The reference answer stays the same for every attempt. The next prompt shows how the answer you edited enters the handoff.

Use the source IDs to check claims, make one correction that affects the next decision, and export the result when you have a useful handoff. The same method works with your own material in a live recipe when you choose to send it.

## Decision memo

[Open the decision memo case](/studio/practice/professional#decision-memo)

An operations lead has to decide how to collect service requests. The packet includes two weeks of intake counts, trial constraints, and three options. The flawed recommendation says the form reduced follow-up by ten percentage points and proposes making it mandatory.

In your revision, say that 14 of 40 email requests and 5 of 20 form requests needed follow-up. The channels had different, self-selected users and unknown request types. Preserve that limit when comparing the current process, a one-team default, and an all-team mandate.

The reference memo recommends a two-week pilot for one team, keeps the urgent phone route, and gives the help desk lead measures and a stop condition. A handoff passes the rubric when a reader can tell who can approve the trial, what they will observe, when to stop, and what could justify a wider trial.

## Evidence brief

[Open the evidence brief case](/studio/practice/professional#evidence-brief)

A documentation manager needs to summarize automated publishing checks. The packet compares 76 of 100 passing pages in April with 91 of 100 in May. It describes a different mix of pages, a template change, a staff refresher, and the scope of the checklist.

Correct the claim that the template improved accessibility by fifteen percentage points. Fifteen points is the observed difference in automated passes. The source packet cannot isolate the effect of the template or establish keyboard and screen-reader acceptance.

The reference brief cites each supplied source near the claim it supports and proposes comparing similar pages and conducting direct checks. A handoff passes the rubric when every reported number can be traced, the causal conclusion is bounded, and the next checks distinguish automated results from human use.

## Document review

[Open the document review case](/studio/practice/professional#document-review)

A support lead is revising the handoff procedure for active severity-one incidents. The packet includes two short draft passages, the controlling policy, and the tools the team already has. The flawed edits transfer ownership on posting and page the wrong role after fifteen minutes.

Use the policy to correct both errors. The outgoing engineer remains responsible until acknowledgement. If no acknowledgement arrives after five minutes, page the duty manager.

The reference review quotes the two draft passages, gives replacements, and rehearses both a timely acknowledgement and a missed acknowledgement. A handoff passes the rubric when an engineer can follow each path without guessing who owns the incident or whom to page.

## Reuse the method with your own work

Give each supplied source a short ID. Ask the first step to map claims or findings to those IDs. Use a review pause before the final recommendation, then inspect the next prompt to confirm your correction carries forward. Download the final answer for handoff, the trace for inspection, or run JSON for restore. Check which source material belongs in any shared export.
