Classroom Challenges · Grade 5

Rescue rainy recess

The class has to stay at their desks. The draft says to throw a paper ball. Which clue did it miss?

Before class

Open an installed copy of Studio on the teacher computer. From Home, choose Try a classroom challenge, then Rescue rainy recess. Project the challenge or read it aloud.

Plan for about 10-15 minutes. The answers are prepared and the app makes no model requests during this challenge.

On paper. Give students page 2 and a pencil. Have partners read the clues, then cover the draft until they’ve picked a game. They can write a fix and copy it into the next prompt.

Lead the activity

  1. Pick a game. Read the clues. Ask partners which game fits the rules and why. Then reveal the first answer and find the words it passes into the next prompt.
  2. Check the draft. Show the paper basketball suggestion. Ask which clue it missed. Agree on one sentence that fixes it.
  3. Find your fix. Enter the class’s sentence and choose Save my fix. Have a student point to the exact words in the next prompt.
  4. Compare ideas. Reveal the prepared example. Ask which clues back up each version and whether both ideas could work.

Show what carries forward

Sample wording for the teacher. Use the class’s sentence during the activity.

Clue

Everyone stays at their desk. No throwing.

Sample fix

Let’s draw robots at our desks.

Next prompt excerpt

Let’s draw robots at our desks. Write a quick announcement with simple directions.

Ask before you finish

“Which words went into the next prompt? What new job did that prompt give the AI?”
