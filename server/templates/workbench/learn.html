A little understanding goes a long way

One answer becomes
the next starting point.

That’s a prompt chain. Small instructions, connected by the answers they produce.

01

Give it a question.

Pick a recipe and tell it what you want to understand or make. Add the audience and any context that matters. Save your own recipe whenever you find a method worth repeating.

02

Let the answer travel.

The first answer becomes part of the second prompt. In a recipe, {% raw %}{{output[-1]}}{% endraw %} means “put the previous answer here.” Open any saved step to see the exact prompt it received.

03

Keep your judgment.

A chain can help organise work. It can also carry an error from one step into the next. Pause, check an answer, edit it, and continue when it makes sense. An AI critique does not establish truth.

When you want live answers

A small setup.

Recipes and the Moon walkthrough work immediately. Live generation uses the OpenRouter settings in your local .env file.

  1. Copy .env.example to .env if you do not already have one.
  2. Set OPENROUTER_API_KEY to your key and OPENROUTER_MODELS to your chosen model IDs, separated by commas.
  3. Restart with ./pc. Choose a model in your recipe, prepare a run, and preview its first prompt.

Model names stay in configuration. The app never asks you to paste credentials into a form or a chain. Your existing key file stays private.

Live requests send your inputs and referenced earlier answers to OpenRouter and the selected provider. Output token limits bound each answer; they are not a dollar spending cap. The workbench sends each step once and leaves retries to you.

Less reaching, less typing

Make room for your way of working.

Alt + N
Choose a new recipe
Alt + L
Open your library
Ctrl / ⌘ + S
Save a recipe in the editor
Ctrl / ⌘ + Enter
Prepare a chain, or start its next step

All fields accept system dictation. Buttons have text labels and visible keyboard focus. Recipes keep recurring instructions so a new run usually needs only a topic.

Editor drafts are saved in this browser as you type. Use Save recipe or Prepare chain to keep work on the computer and include it in workspace backups.

It’s fine to stop

Come back without starting over.

Runs save in a local SQLite database after every completed step. Leaving a page does not cancel a running chain. “Pause after this step” finishes the current request, saves the answer, and stops before another request. Closing the app normally does the same.

If the process stops abruptly, the next startup marks unfinished work as interrupted. It keeps completed answers and waits for you. The provider may have processed an unfinished request; check its usage before choosing Retry. Imported backups never run on their own.
